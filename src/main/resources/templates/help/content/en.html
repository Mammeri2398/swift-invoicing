rocket_launchGetting started

Swift Invoicing manages the invoices, credit notes, quotes and pro forma invoices of an Algerian business, whether self-employed or a company. It runs in the browser, on the PC where it is installed and on the other PCs in the office.

To get off to a good start, in this order:

  1. Fill in the company profile: status, NIF, address, logo. This information is printed on every invoice.
  2. Set up email sending if you want to send invoices by email.
  3. Add your clients and, if you wish, your products and services to the catalogue.
  4. Create accounts for your colleagues in Users.
  5. Create your first invoice: New invoice.

Each page is only shown to users who have the matching right: if a menu is missing, ask your administrator for it.

storefrontCompany profile

In Settings > Company, first choose your status:

  • Self-employed: no VAT. The invoice shows your self-employed (ANAE) card number, your NIF and your NIS, and the statement “VAT not applicable”. The dashboard tracks your turnover against the annual ceiling you enter.
  • Company (SARL, EURL, SPA…): VAT per line (19%, 9% or exempt). The invoice shows the legal form, the share capital, the RC, the NIF, the NIS and the tax article (AI).

Add your logo (PNG or JPEG, 500 KB at most): it replaces your initials at the top of invoices.

An issued invoice keeps the company details as they were on the day it was issued: changing the profile afterwards only affects new invoices and drafts.

groupsClients

A client is either an individual or a business. For a business, the NIF is required before you can issue an invoice; if your own business is a company, the client's RC and tax article are required too.

The clients list shows what each client still owes. The wallet icon opens their account statement: invoices, credit notes and payments, with the balance, ready to print.

A client who has invoices cannot be deleted: their documents must remain available.

inventory_2Catalogue

The catalogue stores your products and services: description, unit, price and VAT rate.

On an invoice, type the start of a line's description: matching catalogue items are suggested. Picking one fills in the unit, the price, the VAT and a quantity of 1. You can then change these values for this invoice only.

Editing or deleting an item never changes an existing invoice: the line keeps its values.

receipt_longCreating and issuing an invoice

  1. New invoice: choose the client, the date, the due date if any and the payment method, then add the lines. Totals are calculated as you type.
  2. Save: the invoice is a draft. It has no number and can still be edited or deleted.
  3. Check it, then click Issue. It then receives its number (FA-2026-0001, then FA-2026-0002…) and can no longer be edited.

Numbers follow each other with no gaps, and start again at 0001 every year. Credit notes are numbered AV-…, quotes DV-… and pro forma invoices PF-….

Stamp duty

When the payment method is Cash, stamp duty is added to the total: 1% up to 30,000 DA, 1.5% up to 100,000 DA, 2% above that, with a minimum of 5 DA, rounded up to the next dinar. The invoice then shows the “Net amount due”. These rates are set in the configuration; check them with your accountant.

A mistake on an issued invoice is corrected with a credit note (see Credit notes), never by editing it.

picture_as_pdfPDF and sending by email

On an invoice's page, the PDF button downloads the document in A4 format, ready to print or send. A draft is marked “BROUILLON” (draft), and a cancelled invoice is marked “ANNULÉ” (cancelled).

The Send by email button prepares an email to the client with the PDF attached: check the address, the subject and the message, then send it. The sending is recorded in the invoice's history.

Documents meant for clients (PDFs and emails) are always in French, whatever the user's language.

paymentsPayments and reminders

On an issued invoice, record each payment you receive: amount, date, method and reference (cheque number, transfer number…). Partial payments are allowed; the invoice becomes Paid when the balance reaches zero. Pay balance records a payment of the amount still due in one click.

An issued invoice that is past its due date and not fully paid is overdue: the dashboard flags it. From the invoice, Send reminder prepares a reminder email with the amount still due.

A payment entered by mistake can be deleted from the invoice.

undoCredit notes and cancelling

A credit note cancels all or part of an issued or paid invoice: a price error, a discount, a return. From the invoice, Create credit note creates a draft credit note with the invoice's lines: adjust them, then issue it. Its amount cannot exceed what is left to cancel on the invoice.

Cancel marks an issued invoice as entirely cancelled, without a credit note; this is no longer possible once it has a credit note. A draft, on the other hand, is simply deleted.

For clear accounts, prefer a credit note to cancelling: ask your accountant which rule applies to your business.

request_quoteQuotes and pro forma invoices

The Quotes page groups quotes and pro forma invoices. They are created and issued like an invoice; the “Valid until” date shows how long they remain valid.

  • An issued quote is marked Accepted or Declined depending on the client's answer.
  • Invoice it turns an issued or accepted quote, or an issued pro forma invoice, into a draft invoice with the same lines and the quote's reference.

A quote or a pro forma invoice is not an invoice: it does not count towards turnover and cannot be marked as paid.

monitoringDashboard and exports

The dashboard shows the year's turnover (issued and paid invoices), the amount still to be collected, drafts, overdue invoices and, for the self-employed, the annual ceiling: an alert appears at 80%.

For a company, the VAT page prepares the month's G50 declaration: turnover excluding VAT and VAT collected per rate, exempt transactions, stamp duty collected and the list of documents, on invoicing or on payments received, with an Excel export. Have the amounts checked by your accountant before declaring.

The Turnover page breaks down each month of the year, with the VAT collected for a company.

From the invoices list, the Excel and CSV buttons export the invoices shown by the filter, for example for your accountant.

manage_accountsUsers and rights

Each person has their own account. In Users, the administrator creates an account with a profile; a temporary password is shown only once. Pass it on to the person, who will change it the first time they sign in.

A profile groups privileges: view invoices, issue them, manage clients, the catalogue, backups… For example, create an “Accounting” profile that can view but not issue. A user's privileges change the next time they sign in.

When someone leaves the business, disable their account rather than deleting it: their name stays in the invoice history.

shield_lockSigning in and security

  • After 5 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes. An inactive session is closed after 20 minutes.
  • Forgot your password? (on the sign-in page) sends a link, valid for 60 minutes, to the account's email: email sending must be set up. Otherwise, an administrator can reset the password from Users.
  • Two-factor authentication adds a 6-digit code from an app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…). Turn it on in My account and keep the recovery codes in a safe place.

translateLanguage

The application is available in French, English and Arabic. Each user chooses their own in My account; it only changes for them. Before signing in, the sign-in page offers all three languages.

Invoices and emails sent to clients stay in French.

backupBackups

Installed on your PC, the application backs up all your data once a day and keeps the last 30 automatic backups. In Settings > Backups:

  • use Back up now before an important operation;
  • regularly download a backup to a USB stick or an external drive, or choose a folder on an external drive: a PC failure must not take your invoices with it;
  • Restore replaces all the data with the data from the backup (type RESTORE to confirm). A backup of the current data is made just before, and everyone is signed out;
  • on a new PC, Import a backup file, then restore it.

A red banner warns the administrator if a backup fails or if there has been none for 3 days.

workspace_premiumLicence

A new installation runs for 30 days as a trial version, for 3 users. After that, paste the licence key you received from the publisher into Settings > Licence: it sets the number of active users and the end date, and it is tied to the company's NIF.

A banner warns you 30 days before the end. After that date, everything keeps working for 15 days; then the application becomes read-only: your invoices can still be viewed, downloaded and backed up until a new key is activated.

lanNetwork and email

The other PCs in the office open the application in their browser at the address shown in About (for example http://OFFICE-PC:7070). The PC where it is installed must be switched on, and the firewall must allow it (an option during installation).

To send invoices, reminders and forgotten-password links, set up the outgoing mail server in Settings > Email (Gmail: smtp.gmail.com, port 587, with an app password), then send yourself a test email.

quizFrequently asked questions

I issued an invoice with a mistake.

Create a credit note for that invoice, then a new, correct invoice. An issued invoice cannot be edited: this is what guarantees an unbroken sequence of numbers.

I have moved from self-employed to company.

Change the status in the company profile. Invoices already issued keep their old status; new ones apply VAT.

The button I need does not appear.

Your profile does not have the matching privilege: ask the administrator for it.

The administrator has forgotten their password and email is not set up.

Another administrator can reset it from Users. If there is no other administrator, contact support.

A client disputes that an invoice is genuine.

The public “Verify an invoice” page (at the bottom of every page) confirms that a number exists, with its date and status, without revealing the client or the amount.
